Camera research safeguards

This preview does not request camera access, collect consent, or generate biometric readings. The camera feature is deliberately inactive.

  • ✓Passkeys use the phone’s built-in authenticator. The fingerprint image or template is not shared with BioPay.
  • ✓Any future rPPG or eye-measurement study must be separately opt-in, clearly explained, and independently validated.
  • ✓Camera-derived research measurements must not approve, deny, or price a payment.
  • ✓Consent, retention, access, deletion, and incident-response rules need a server-side record and formal review before a real pilot.
